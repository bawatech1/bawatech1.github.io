Biography

I am Bawaya Kenneth, a web developer and technology specialist based in Wakiso District, Uganda.

I was born and raised in Nangabo Village, within Kasangati Town Council, Wakiso District. My interest in technology began with computers in 2015. In 2023 I started to explore software and the web more deliberately, and since then I have been building practical skills in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, creating and maintaining websites, installing and configuring software, and troubleshooting computers.

I work under the professional brand BAWA TECHS. Alongside my technical work, I have skills in sales and customer service, and I use the Microsoft Office applications in my day-to-day work.

My long-term goal is to become a skilled software engineer. I am at an early stage of that journey, and I would rather earn expertise through consistent study and practice than claim it too soon. My official website, linked below, shows my work and my current project in more detail.
